Bio

I am a ML Research Resident in the Visual GenAI team at Yandex Research, where I work on accelerating diffusion models and improving their generation quality under the supervision of Dmitry Baranchuk and Artem Babenko. At the same time, I am pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Science at HSE University and studying at the Yandex School of Data Analysis.

I am looking for PhD opportunities for 2027.

Previously, I was a Research Intern at the Centre of Deep Learning and Bayesian Methods at HSE University, working on face swapping with diffusion models and GANs, supervised by Aibek Alanov. I received my Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from HSE University.
